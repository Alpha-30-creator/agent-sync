# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree about the work, not about
the person doing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback about code, decisions and behaviour — not about people.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. This project has a written decision log
  (`docs/design/decisions/`) precisely so that "no" comes with a reason you can argue
  with.
- Bug reports and questions are welcome from people at any level of experience. "I don't
  understand what this does" is useful information about the documentation.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, or persistent unwelcome contact. Publishing someone's
private information. Deliberately derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer through a private GitHub security advisory or a direct message.
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, and
may block accounts, without prior notice where the behaviour is clear.
